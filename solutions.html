Industrial Equipment Solutions

From technical consultation to equipment delivery, KMD provides reliable motor and pump solutions for industrial projects worldwide.

Our Services

Integrated support across the industrial equipment supply process, from technical requirements to final delivery.

01

Technical Solution Consulting

Our industry specialists provide technical consulting for motor and pump selection, application requirements, energy efficiency considerations, and equipment recommendations across industrial projects.

02

Procurement Services

Working with trusted international brands including WEG, VEM, and Xylem, KMD handles the full procurement process — sourcing, vendor qualification, commercial negotiation, purchase order management, and expediting to ensure competitive pricing and on-time supply.

03

Project Management

Dedicated project managers oversee the full project lifecycle — coordinating with manufacturers, managing timelines, handling inspection and factory acceptance tests (FAT), and ensuring complete documentation including certifications and compliance reports.

04

Equipment Delivery

KMD manages the complete export and logistics chain — from freight coordination and customs clearance to shipping documentation and final delivery to project sites worldwide.
