WHO WE ARE

Industrial Equipment Partner with Proven Project Experience

Founded in 2016, Kamaide Technology Co., Ltd. (KMD) is an industrial equipment company specializing in electric motors, industrial pumps, and related equipment for international projects.

With extensive experience supporting industrial projects across diverse applications and markets, KMD provides practical support for equipment selection, technical clarification, sourcing, documentation, and project coordination.

Through established supply channels and cooperation with recognized international manufacturers, KMD supports the supply and delivery of industrial equipment from initial requirements through final delivery, helping customers manage procurement with greater efficiency and reliability.
